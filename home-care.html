The 5-Step Proactive Maintenance Cycle

1. Intake & Baseline Assessment

Before service begins, a Restoricon technician conducts a thorough property walkthrough — documenting rooflines, siding, gutters, HVAC drains, sump pumps, foundation grading, and moisture indicators to create your home's initial condition baseline.

2. Exterior Envelope & Roofline Protection

We keep your building envelope watertight with scheduled gutter cleanings, downspout clearing, window and exterior door seal checks, and siding evaluations. Higher tiers include soft washing and driveway pressure washing to prevent biological buildup.

3. Regular Lawn & Grounds Care

Under Plus, Complete, and Estate packages, your grounds receive weekly or bi-weekly mowing, sharp edging along driveways and walkways, string trimming, hard-surface blow-offs, and complete seasonal spring and fall cleanups.

4. Critical Mechanical & Freeze Checks

We inspect sump pump operation, clear HVAC condensate drain lines, clean dryer vents to reduce fire hazards, and winterize hose bibs and exterior water lines before Connecticut freezing weather arrives.

5. Digital Home Health Report & Early Action

After every visit, you receive a detailed digital Home Health Report with color-coded observations (Green / Yellow / Orange / Red), timestamped photos, completed tasks, and clear recommendations before minor concerns escalate into major damage.

Program Scope & Operating Transparency

Predictable Preventive Labor: Restoricon HomeCare is a recurring property maintenance and visual assessment program designed to help homeowners prevent avoidable deterioration. It is not an insurance policy, home warranty, or unlimited repair contract.

Materials & Replacement Parts: Membership fees cover scheduled labor and defined inspection scopes. Materials, consumables, custom items, and replacement parts (e.g. specialty filters, batteries, plumbing fittings, electrical components) are billed separately at direct cost with prior homeowner authorization whenever practical.

Damage Discovery Protocol: If a technician identifies unexpected damage (e.g., hidden moisture, structural deflection, roof storm damage), work in the affected zone pauses and the homeowner is notified immediately with photo documentation and options for a separate repair quote or emergency builder response.
