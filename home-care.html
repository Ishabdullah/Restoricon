How Property Complexity is Measured — 25-Point Assessment

During your initial baseline walkthrough, our lead technician scores five physical property factors (1 to 5 points each) to confirm your exact plan tier. The 25-point score maps to tiers as follows: 5–8 = Tier A, 9–12 = Tier B, 13–16 = Tier C, 17–20 = Tier D, 21–25 = Tier E. If your complexity score places you in a higher tier than your lot size, the higher tier governs.

1. Lawn & Turf Complexity

Mowable turf square footage, terrain slopes, obstacle density (beds, fences, pool, retaining walls), and edging requirements.

2. House Size & Architecture

Total conditioned square footage, number of stories (1, 1.5, 2, 2.5, 3+), roofline valleys, and exterior wash accessibility.

3. Gutters & Drainage Scope

Linear footage of gutter runs, story heights, presence of gutter guards, tree proximity, and downspout discharge paths.

4. Seasonal Leaf & Debris Load

Deciduous mature tree canopy coverage and estimated seasonal spring/fall cleanup volume for both visits.

5. Access & Driveway Area

Driveway square footage and surface type (asphalt, concrete, pavers), gate widths, and wash equipment water hookup access.

The 5-Step Proactive Maintenance Cycle

1. Intake & Baseline Assessment

Before service begins, a Restoricon technician conducts a thorough property walkthrough — documenting rooflines, siding, gutters, HVAC drains, sump pumps, foundation grading, and moisture indicators. We record your Wash Scope Baseline (surface area, included structures) and complete the 25-point complexity scoring to assign your exact plan tier. This is your home's permanent condition baseline — every future visit is compared against it.

2. Exterior Envelope & Roofline Protection

We keep your building envelope watertight with scheduled gutter cleanings, downspout clearing, window and exterior door seal checks, and full siding evaluations from ground level. Complete and Estate tiers include annual whole-house soft washing and driveway pressure washing to prevent biological buildup, extend surface life, and maintain curb appeal. All wash work is performed against your recorded Wash Scope Baseline — no scope creep, no surprise invoices.

3. Regular Lawn & Grounds Care

Under Plus, Complete, and Estate packages, your grounds receive bi-weekly or weekly mowing (April 15 – October 31), sharp precision edging along driveways and walkways, string trimming, hard-surface blow-offs, and complete spring and fall seasonal cleanups. The mowing season may shift up to three weeks in either direction based on actual growing conditions. Visits missed due to drought dormancy or saturated ground are not carried over.

4. Critical Mechanical & Freeze Protection Checks

We inspect sump pump operation and float function, clear HVAC condensate drain lines, clean dryer vents to reduce fire hazards, and winterize hose bibs and exterior water lines before Connecticut's freezing temperatures arrive. We also perform a water heater area and visible plumbing leak review, basement and crawlspace moisture check, and visual smoke and CO detector test — the small checks that catch big problems early.

5. Digital Home Health Report & Early Action

After every seasonal assessment, you receive a detailed digital Home Health Report with color-coded observations — Green (all clear), Yellow (monitor), Orange (address soon), Red (urgent action needed) — plus timestamped photos, completed task records, and clear recommendations. Routine visit Service Visit Records are delivered within 1–3 business days. Your full report history is accessible through the Customer Portal.

Program Scope & Operating Transparency

Program Nature: Restoricon HomeCare is a recurring property maintenance and visual assessment program designed to help homeowners prevent avoidable deterioration. It is not an insurance policy, home warranty, or unlimited repair contract.

Visual Assessments (Not a Home Inspection): Assessments are visual reviews performed by maintenance technicians from safe, accessible locations. They are not performed by a licensed Connecticut home inspector, do not comply with home inspection standards of practice, and cannot detect latent, concealed or inaccessible conditions. The Home Health Report must not be used or represented as a home inspection report in any real estate transaction.

Connecticut Sales Tax: All prices published on this site are exclusive of Connecticut sales tax. Sales tax is added and shown separately on every invoice as required by the CT Department of Revenue Services.

Materials & Replacement Parts: Membership fees cover scheduled labor and defined inspection scopes. Materials, consumables, and replacement parts are billed at direct cost plus a 25% sourcing fee, with prior homeowner authorization via Form 7.

Insurance & Compliance: Restoricon, LLC is a registered Connecticut Home Improvement Contractor (CT HIC.0706905) located at 458 Somers Road, Ellington, CT 06029. We maintain commercial general liability, workers’ compensation, and care, custody, and control coverage. Certificates of insurance are available upon request.
