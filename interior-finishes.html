How We Approach Interior Finish Work

1. Assess What's Underneath

Before any finish work starts, we assess the condition of what's already there — subfloor, existing hardwood, cabinet boxes, wall surfaces. That determines whether we're refinishing, repairing, or replacing, so the finished result holds up instead of covering up a problem.

2. Floors Sanded and Refinished On Site

Existing hardwood is sanded down to bare wood, then stained and sealed in multiple coats for a durable, even finish. It's a dust-controlled process, and we protect the rest of the home the same way we do on every job — drop cloths, containment, and clean pathways in and out.

3. Cabinetry, Trim & Paint

Cabinets are refinished or replaced, trim is repaired or installed to match the rest of the home, and walls are painted with a level of prep and coverage that lasts — no shortcuts on sanding, priming, or cut lines.

4. Final Walkthrough

Once the finish work is complete, we walk the space with you room by room — checking floors, trim, cabinetry, and paint against the agreed scope before we call the job done. Nothing gets signed off until it meets our standard and yours.
